# Notice and attribution

This corpus contains **derivative works** — robot trajectories produced by optimising against
human motion capture and its contact annotations. It is not a redistribution of any source
dataset's own files, but the sources' terms still govern it.

Released under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**: the most restrictive term among the sources, propagated.
Non-commercial use only; derivatives must carry the same licence.
